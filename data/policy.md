# Acme Health Plan (fictional) — Durable Medical Equipment

> **FICTIONAL — DEMO ONLY.** Acme Health Plan does not exist. This document was written for a hackathon demo and is not a real insurance policy.

## §4.2 Power Mobility Devices

**§4.2(a) Functional need.** A power mobility device (power wheelchair) is covered when the member cannot functionally self-propel an optimally configured manual wheelchair to carry out daily activities in the home, such as moving between the bedroom, bathroom and kitchen.

**§4.2(b) Home suitability.** The member's home must allow the device to be used. The device must fit through the doorways the member needs to use daily and must be able to turn in the available hallway or room space. A home assessment documenting doorway widths and turning space must be submitted.

**§4.2(c) Clinical documentation.** The request must include a face-to-face examination note from the treating clinician, dated within 45 days of the request, and a signed clinician statement describing the member's mobility limitations in functional terms.

**§4.2(d) Prior authorisation.** Prior authorisation must be obtained before the device is dispensed. Requests without prior authorisation will be denied.

**§4.2(e) Least costly alternative.** Where a less costly device would meet the member's functional needs in the home, the plan covers the less costly device. The plan's standard alternative is a standard manual wheelchair.

## §4.3 Member cost share

**§4.3(a)** Covered durable medical equipment is subject to a **20% member cost share after the deductible**.

## §7 Appeals

**§7.1** A member may appeal a coverage decision **within 60 days of the date on the denial notice**.

**§7.2** An appeal should include any new evidence that addresses the reason for denial, for example measurements, home assessments or clinician statements.

**§7.3** Acme will make a decision **within 30 days of receiving a complete appeal**.
