# Milo demo film — three storylines

The one thing: **Milo lets someone who can't easily type, click or speak get a real task done — and shows every step.**
One viewer, one task: Maria (fictional) gets the right power wheelchair approved, from a denial letter to a filed appeal.
One piece of data travels through it: the letters **I · W · W** → "I want water", then **W · W · A** → her appeal.

Assumptions: 2:30–3:00, 16:9 1920×1080 @ 30 fps, soft voiceover (ElevenLabs "Sarah", free fallback: browser/OS voice), burned-in captions, quiet music (free/royalty-free) plus the app's own soft chimes. All on-screen data labelled "demo data".

---

## A. "Three moves" — a moment in someone's day · one frame that rebuilds  ⭐ recommended

Why: a stranger instantly gets it — a real face, three head movements, a sentence spoken aloud — then the same tiny motion drives a whole insurance fight.

- **What you see/feel:** Real webcam footage: a face, a glowing nose dot on a grid, three small head moves. "I want water" is spoken in a warm voice and fills the screen. Then the same nose moves take on a denial letter and win.
- **First 2 seconds:** Close-up of the face with the orange nose dot and 6×5 grid; the letter **I** lights up on the board as the head tilts. No logo, no title.
- **Beats:** hook (I·W·W → "I want water" spoken, "3 moves · saved 9 letters") → tension (denial letter: "not medically necessary") → reveal (Milo hops in, name lands) → proof: *ask* (W·W·A → deadline) · *check* (2 ✅ / 6 ❌, Terra 27″+2″ > 28″ door) · *draft* (appeal doc builds) · *challenge* (strict reviewer, 2 objections → v2) · *verify & nod* (✓ → filed, confetti) → payoff (Receipts trail populates: mode, confidence, evidence, live sponsors) → claim "Works with whatever you can do" → end card.
- **Look:** the app's own world — warm cream, white cards, burnt-orange accent, Lexend; real screens pushed to 1.6–2×; the webcam as a rounded window.
- **Sound:** soft lo-fi bed, the app's real chimes on every selection, calm voiceover.

## B. "Four ways in" — the same question four ways · split screen to one

- **What you see/feel:** Four panes ask Milo the same thing — typing, speaking, a nose pointer, fingerspelling — then collapse into one answer. Inclusion made literal.
- **First 2 seconds:** Four panes at once, each showing a different person-input starting "Where's my…".
- **Beats:** hook (4 inputs) → collapse to one answer → tension (denial) → proof (fit → appeal → file, faster) → payoff (receipts show each mode chip) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** cream ground, each pane tinted by its mode colour, merging into the app.
- **Sound:** four soft overlapping voices resolving into one chord.

## C. "Inside Milo's eye" — the mascot carries the story · endless zoom (bold)

- **What you see/feel:** The camera dives into Milo's pixel eye, which becomes the letter grid; a letter cell becomes a fit-table cell; a cell becomes the appeal page. Playful 8-bit, but every screen is real.
- **First 2 seconds:** Milo blinks, full-frame pixels; on the blink the eye becomes the 6×5 board with a nose cursor.
- **Beats:** hook (zoom into the eye) → letters → sentence → zoom into fit table → zoom into appeal → reviewer → nod → confetti pixels → end card.
- **Look:** pixel grid as the material, the app's cream/orange palette.
- **Sound:** soft chiptune, pixel blips on each zoom.
